   computes `copies` by counting TRUE platform sources, i.e.
   `Object.values(sources)` over the four kubo/pinata/filebase/filecoin flags
   (cid-view.ts:561-564) — a 0..4 count, not a provider-row count. A duplicate
   megaphone peer (Source IPNI + Source Amino DHT) classifies to filebase in both

   non-blocking nit: a test documenting that a duplicated peer across sources
